# Code of Conduct

The Smithy is a technical workshop. Participation must remain respectful, specific, and grounded in the work.

We welcome disagreement about architecture, implementation, design, and evidence. We do not accept harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory behavior, doxxing, threats, or deliberate disruption.

When feedback is difficult, address the artifact, decision, or observed behavior—not the person. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard.
